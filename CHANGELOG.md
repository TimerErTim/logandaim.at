### Added
- Account screens for registration, sign-in, and password recovery.
- An administration area for Anlagen, including staff roles and Parkour setup with Pflocks and

- A public Parkour list that needs no sign-in.
- A Runden view: run a Runde and see its Wertungen per Ziel and for the whole Runde.
